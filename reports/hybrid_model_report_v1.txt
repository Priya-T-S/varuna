CAVEATS
  - Extreme (>=204.4 mm) is not measurable: 1 such region-day(s) survive into the fusion dataset and none fall in the held-out test period. Every Extreme figure in this report is structural, not evidence the class works. Needs IMD gridded / GPM IMERG data (already flagged for Phases 3 and 4).
  - The comparison is underpowered: selection rests on 592 validation region-days and the verdict on 623 test region-days holding only 34 high-impact day(s). Differences of a few macro-F1 points here are noise, not evidence one approach is better.
  - Fusion did NOT beat the single-modality baseline on the held-out test period: weather_only scored macro-F1 0.693 against the fusion's 0.688. The fusion wins on validation and loses on test, which is the signature of selection overfitting on a split this small.
  - The selected fusion is conservative in the wrong direction: it recalls only 53% of Heavy days while raising overall accuracy. For an early-warning system a missed event costs more than a false alarm, so macro-F1 selection should be revisited against a recall- or cost-weighted criterion.
  - weather_risk_score is generated out-of-fold by walk-forward retraining (6 folds): every region-day is scored by a Phase 3 estimator fitted only on strictly earlier days, giving AUC 0.930 train / 0.921 validation / 0.904 test. Scoring with the shipped Phase 3 artifact instead measured AUC 1.000 on train and validation against 0.875 on test — memorisation, which made all trained approaches tie at a meaningless validation macro-F1 of 1.000.
  - satellite_risk_score is left in-sample: the Phase 4 checkpoint saw the fusion training scenes, but its measured AUC is stable across splits (0.909 train / 0.884 validation / 0.863 test), so it shows no comparable memorisation. Walk-forward retraining of the CNN was not run for this phase.
  - The satellite branch classifies day T while the target is day T+1, so it contributes current convective state rather than a forecast; its standalone score on this task is correspondingly weaker than its Phase 4 nowcast score.
  - Fusion is limited to the 3963 region-days that have both modalities and an out-of-fold weather score — satellite coverage, not weather coverage, is the binding constraint.
